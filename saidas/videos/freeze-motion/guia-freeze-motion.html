SCENE 01

Suspended Above the City

Bullet-Time Urbano
📸 Step 1: Image Prompt (Foto Âncora) GPT Image / Midjourney
Create a photorealistic cinematic freeze-frame using @img as the exact male identity reference. Preserve his facial structure, hairstyle, skin tone, facial hair, age, and body proportions without beautification. Show him suspended high above a city street, caught in a dramatic falling pose. His torso leans backward, one open hand reaches toward the camera with fingers spread, and one bent leg projects toward the lens, making the shoe sole appear large in the foreground. His other leg extends diagonally outward. His mouth is open in surprise. Dress him in a plain white crew-neck T-shirt, blue jeans, tan rubber-soled casual shoes, and black sunglasses. Far below, show an asphalt street with lane markings, parked cars, tiny pedestrians, leafy trees, and tall stone buildings. Suspend a smartphone, keys, loose coins, and white papers around him at different depths. Shoot from above, looking steeply downward with a 24mm wide-angle lens. Keep his face recognizable, with perspective exaggeration concentrated on the reaching hand and foreground shoe. Bright midday sunlight, realistic shadows, natural colors, crisp textures, every object frozen sharply.
🚫 Negative Prompt
Different identity, altered facial features, changed hairstyle, distorted face, extra fingers, extra limbs, duplicate person, malformed hands, warped buildings, motion blur, illustration, blood, injuries, text, captions, logos, watermarks, interface elements.
🎬 Step 2: Animation Prompt (Image-to-Video) VOSU / Veo / Kling
Create a cinematic bullet-time shot using the reference image. Everything in the scene must remain completely frozen in time throughout the entire video. The male character must remain perfectly frozen in the exact falling pose shown in the reference, with absolutely no body movement, no head movement, no facial movement, no blinking, no breathing animation, no hair movement, no clothing movement and no change in expression. The smartphone, keys, coins, papers and every other airborne object must remain completely suspended and locked in their exact positions. The cars, pedestrians, trees, buildings, street and entire city environment below must also remain completely frozen. The character must not continue falling or move downward. Only the camera moves. Smoothly orbit the camera around the completely frozen character in a cinematic 180-degree arc during the full duration of the shot. Start directly from the front of the character, move naturally around one side, reach the 90-degree side profile around the middle of the video, then continue around and finish directly behind the character at 180 degrees near the end. Use a controlled medium-slow orbital speed — clearly moving throughout the shot, but never fast, rushed or extremely slow. Keep the speed smooth and consistent from beginning to end. The camera must physically travel around the character in 3D space while maintaining a consistent distance and keeping him as the fixed center point. The changing camera perspective should create strong realistic 3D parallax between the frozen phone, keys, coins, character and distant city below. The character's face and identity must remain exactly consistent with the reference. Maintain the exact clothing, pose, proportions, lighting and object positions. Photorealistic cinematic lighting, realistic depth of field, dramatic height perspective, high-end Hollywood cinematography, Matrix-style bullet-time effect, ultra-stable camera movement, one continuous seamless shot, no cuts.
⏱️ CAMERA TIMING: FRONT → 45° → SIDE 90° → 135° → BACK 180°. Medium-slow constant speed. ZERO scene motion except camera.
